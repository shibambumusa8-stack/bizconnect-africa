Trust & safety

Shop with more confidence

Buyer protection should be built into every eligible distance order.

🪪 Verified sellersCheck seller identity and business details, with badges that explain what was checked.
📦 Trackable deliveryUse supported courier tracking and delivery evidence. A tracking number alone is not proof that the right customer received the parcel.
🛡️ Buyer ProtectionEligible orders can follow a clear dispute process if items never arrive, arrive damaged, or materially differ from the listing.

How a protected order works

1. Pay securely
Use an approved payment flow; avoid paying sellers privately for protected orders.
2. Seller dispatches
Seller adds supported courier details and dispatch evidence.
3. Confirm delivery
Courier evidence and a recipient confirmation process help verify receipt.
4. Resolve problems
Open a dispute within the stated time limit and provide evidence for review.
Safety reminder: never share a delivery code before the parcel is physically with you. This demo does not hold funds, verify sellers, track parcels, or process refunds.
